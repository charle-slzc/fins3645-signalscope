## Implementation Decisions

- `src/portfolios.py` owns Phase 1 portfolio logic.
- Method labels are stable: `Equal Weight`, `Minimum Variance`, and
  `Maximum Sharpe`.
- `Equal Weight` is tagged as `benchmark`; the two optimiser methods are tagged
  as `optimisation`.
- Eligibility requires complete finite observations across the full trailing
  estimation window. Missing returns are not zero-filled.
- Monthly rebalance dates are the first possible live date after the estimation
  window, then the first valid observation whenever the calendar month changes.
- Weight decisions use estimation rows ending at `t-1`; the weights first apply
  to return date `t`.
- Turnover is `sum(abs(new_weight - previous_weight))` across the union of old
  and new assets. Initial turnover is measured from cash.
- Transaction cost is `0.001 * turnover`, deducted from net return on the live
  rebalance date.
- Minimum Variance and Maximum Sharpe use `scipy.optimize.minimize` with SLSQP,
  long-only bounds, and a sum-to-one equality constraint.
- Covariance uses the sample covariance. If the matrix is ill-conditioned, a
  deterministic diagonal jitter equal to `mean_variance * 1e-8` is added.
- Solver diagnostics are recorded for every rebalance, including fallback status.
- On optimiser failure or invalid weights, the engine falls back to Equal Weight
  over eligible assets and records the reason.

consistent with unconstrained optimisation over the complete eligible universe
and no forced crypto sleeve.
